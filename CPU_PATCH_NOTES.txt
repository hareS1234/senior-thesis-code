CPU run notes

Main jobs:
  sbatch run_graph_features.sbatch
  sbatch run_ml_regression.sbatch

The GNN job is optional:
  sbatch run_gnn.sbatch

The graph-feature job has lighter presets, resume support, and periodic saves.
The regression job can limit its targets/models and skip forward selection.
The GNN job has CPU settings, early stopping, and saves after each fold.

Current regression defaults:
  targets: log_MFPT_AB, log_t1
  models: OLS, Ridge_1, RidgeCV, RandomForest, GradientBoosting

For a larger run, add log_MFPT_BA, bump the permutation repeats, or turn
forward selection back on for one target.
